About the academy

We started as a tight-knit crew of variety performers who wanted a better way to teach high-risk props: fewer heroics, more preparation, and transparent decision-making in every class.

Our values

Respect for the tool, respect for the room, and respect for the student. We celebrate courage, but never at the cost of fundamentals or peer safety.

What to expect

You will spend meaningful time on warm-ups, prop care, and emergency plans before advanced patterns. Speed follows control; spectacle follows consistency.
